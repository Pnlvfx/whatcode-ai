# WhatCode: The Mobile Companion for OpenCode

You start a long-running coding task, step away from your desk, and your phone buzzes the moment the agent finishes. That's WhatCode.

WhatCode is the mobile companion for [OpenCode](https://opencode.ai), the open-source AI coding agent that runs on your machine. It lets you monitor sessions, send messages, and get push notifications in real time, all from your phone. It's available on iPhone today, with more platforms coming. See the [FAQ](/faq).

**[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/whatcode/id6760623503)**

WhatCode is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenCode.

## What's covered here

- **WhatCode**: the mobile companion. What it does, how to connect it to your machine, and how to navigate your projects and sessions.
- **Daemon**: the lightweight server that bridges the mobile companion and OpenCode, enabling push notifications, better project sorting, remote access and much more features.

## Quick overview

1. Install OpenCode on your machine.
2. Run the WhatCode daemon. It starts OpenCode and exposes it on your local network.
3. Run `whatcode pair` in another terminal, open the WhatCode app, scan the QR code, and you're connected.
4. Start a coding session. When the agent finishes, your phone buzzes.
